BetterNotes

A fast, free and open-source notes app for Windows. Your notes are stored on your device. You can optionally back them up to the hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive.

Download for Windows Privacy policy Source code User guide

What it does

  • Create, search, tag and organise notes, with pictures and reminders.
  • Works fully offline. No account is required.
  • Optional Google Drive backup. BetterNotes requests only the drive.appdata permission, so it can access its own hidden folder and none of your other files.
